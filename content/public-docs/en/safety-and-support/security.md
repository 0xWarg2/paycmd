## Never share secrets

Do not enter a seed phrase, private key, API key, or recovery phrase into Payna or AskPayna. Payna requests MetaMask signatures through the extension and does not need wallet secrets.

## Preview before execution

AI or parsers can understand intent but cannot bypass confirmation. Verify amount, token, rail, chain, recipient, fee, and gas mode. Cancel if the preview differs from the original request.

## Testnet scope

Wallet, faucet, Gateway, bridge, swap, and proof flows documented here target testnets. Do not treat demo balances as production funds or AskPayna answers as financial advice.
